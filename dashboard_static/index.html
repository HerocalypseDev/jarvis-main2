                                  Scheduled work

                                  Things you asked Jarvis to do later. At the time, Jarvis does them itself (every step is in the Audit Trail); dangerous system actions still wait for your yes.

                                    Clipboard history

                                    The last 50 things you copied. Secret-looking copies keep only their length, and copies password managers mark private are never read. Turn off with JARVIS_CLIPBOARD_HISTORY=0.

                                      Voice macros

                                      Say a trigger phrase and the steps run straight away, with no AI call. Steps use Jarvis's existing tools; anything dangerous still needs your yes.

                                        Meeting notes

                                        Off until you start it. Records only what your speakers play (the other people), transcribes it, and writes a summary with action items when it stops. Auto-start for Zoom/Teams/Meet windows is JARVIS_MEETING_AUTO (off by default).
